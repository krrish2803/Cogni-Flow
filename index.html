FAQ

Questions worth asking.

Why not just give the answer faster?

Speed is useful when the goal is completion. Socratic Scaffold is built for the moments where the goal is competence—when you want the learner to be able to solve the next problem independently.

Who is Socratic Scaffold for?

It is designed for students, educators, and learning platforms—especially in coding, mathematics, and other reasoning-heavy subjects.

How is it different from ChatGPT or AI tutors?

Most general AI tools lead with an answer. Socratic Scaffold leads with diagnosis, structured hints, and required learner action, then validates mastery.

Can it work for coding and math?

Yes. These are ideal starting points because each requires transparent reasoning, practice, and the ability to explain why an approach works.

How do teachers benefit?

Teachers get a clearer distinction between submitted work and demonstrated understanding, with visibility into the learner’s reasoning journey.
